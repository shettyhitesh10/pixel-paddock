# Retro racing visual reference

The user clarified that this should look like a retro F1 racing interface, without actual photographs or modern white dashboard styling. Original pixel art and detailed animated characters should lead the experience.

Screenshots reviewed through image search:

- Nintendo F1 Race title and gameplay: https://www.gavas.jp/products/detail.php?product_id=4056 and https://medium.com/swlh/build-an-ai-to-play-f1-race-64f078a3fce2 — dark menu canvas, orange/yellow pixel commands, checkered flags, perspective circuit and instrument strip.
- F1 Pole Position SNES course-selection and HUD: https://retro.gg/game/f1-pole-position/2184 and https://gamefabrique.com/games/f1-pole-position/ — blue framed control panels, bright circuit diagrams, compact standings, mechanical instrumentation.
- Road Rash II character/results screen: https://goaste.cx/goaste/roadrash2.html — detailed shaded character art, blue/yellow menu text, strongly framed results layout.

Applied direction: original bitmap typeface, navy/cobalt HUD panels, yellow timing displays, square pixel borders and selection arrows, dense game-menu layouts, original circuit artwork, and detailed shaded pixel people with six distinct pose frames. All artwork is original; game screenshots are reference material, not embedded site assets.
